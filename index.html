Roster Evolution Analyzer

How trades, acquisitions and player absences shape fantasy-team performance.

What is the Roster Evolution Analyzer?

Fantasy football standings tell us what a team has done over an entire season. But after trades and roster changes, that record may no longer reflect the team a manager actually has today.

This tool looks at how each team performed before and after a roster event. It compares head-to-head results, performance against every other team (all-play), results against the weekly league median, and scoring averages. The goal is to give a clearer picture of current team performance and roster evolution rather than relying only on season-long standings.

These are observed comparisons, not proof that a trade caused improvement or decline. Small samples, schedules, injuries, and other roster moves all matter.
